How to Apply

Follow these steps to complete your application

  1. Create an account with a valid email address and phone number.
  2. Log in to open your application dashboard.
  3. Choose your programme and fill in your details.
  4. Upload your academic documents.
  5. Pay the application fee, then submit and track your status.
